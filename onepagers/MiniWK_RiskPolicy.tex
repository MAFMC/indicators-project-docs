\section{Risk Policy: Potential Indicators and
Pathways}\label{risk-policy-potential-indicators-and-pathways}

\begin{noteblock}
Risk policy is a Council-level decision. Current
\href{http://www.mafmc.org/s/MAFMC-ABC-Control-Rule-White-Paper.pdf}{risk
policy} uses stock status as the only indicator of when to adjust risk
tolerance. Low stock status results in much more risk averse probability
of overfishing and lower ABC relative to OFL, high stock status
(\textgreater1.5 B/Bmsy) is as risk tolerant as allowed by law with ABC
close to OFL.

\end{noteblock}

\textbf{Key question: What additional indicators could inform Council
risk tolerance? Could habitat conditions or socioeconomic indicators in
addition to stock status tell us whether we can be more or less risky?}
