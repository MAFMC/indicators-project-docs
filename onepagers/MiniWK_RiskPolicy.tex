\section{Risk Policy: Potential Indicators and
Pathways}\label{risk-policy-potential-indicators-and-pathways}

\begin{noteblock}
Risk policy is a Council-level decision. Current
\href{https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2020/12/15/2020-27562/fisheries-of-the-northeastern-united-states-omnibus-framework-adjustment-to-modify-the-mid-atlantic}{Risk
Policy} uses stock status as the only indicator of when to adjust risk
tolerance. Low stock status (B/Bmsy \textless{} 1) results in much more
risk averse probability of overfishing and lower ABC relative to OFL,
high stock status (\textgreater1.5 B/Bmsy) is as risk tolerant as
allowed by law with ABC close to OFL.

\end{noteblock}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Key question: What additional indicators could inform Council
  risk tolerance? Could habitat conditions or socioeconomic indicators
  in addition to stock status tell us whether we can be more or less
  risky?}
\item
  \textbf{Scope: Should we develop a general process for using
  indicators in the risk policy, or focus on a specific application for
  a single species? Which species?}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What information is used
now?}\label{what-information-is-used-now}

The current Risk Policy was finalized by the Council in
\href{https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2020/12/15/2020-27562/fisheries-of-the-northeastern-united-states-omnibus-framework-adjustment-to-modify-the-mid-atlantic}{December
2020}. It is based on a
\href{https://static1.squarespace.com/static/511cdc7fe4b00307a2628ac6/t/5e56e0f286208b151ba4f55d/1582752007474/S1_Fine+tuning+the+ABC+control+rule+for+Mid-Atlantic+stocks_Wiedenmann.pdf}{management
strategy evaluation} that compared performance of alternative policies
across life history types represented by summer flounder, scup, and
butterfish.

\vspace{1ex}

\pandocbounded{\includegraphics[keepaspectratio]{MiniWK_RiskPolicy_files/figure-latex/MAFMCriskpolicy-1.pdf}}

\vspace{1ex}

The
\href{http://www.mafmc.org/s/MAFMC-ABC-Control-Rule-White-Paper.pdf}{ABC
control rule} includes an SSC decision on uncertainty in the assessment
and converts the risk policy into an ABC given the stock status.

\pandocbounded{\includegraphics[keepaspectratio]{MiniWK_RiskPolicy_files/figure-latex/OFLCVtoABC-1.pdf}}

\begin{ltpurplebox}
\textbf{Important:} The use of indicators in the SSC's ABC decision
process will be \textbf{considered separately} under Catch/Quota
setting. Indicators here will address the risk policy and adjustment to
risk tolerance rather than assessment uncertainty.

\end{ltpurplebox}

\subsubsection{Potential Pathways}\label{potential-pathways}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Evaluate indicators of favorable or unfavorable habitat or
  socioeconomic conditions for a species or life history type
\item
  Simulate management with different risk tolerance under different
  conditions (update MSE?)
\item
  Compare indicator-based approach vs.~status quo: Which
  indicator-adjusted risk tolerance meets objectives across species
  portfolio or for a selected individual species?
\end{itemize}
