\section{Management Decisions: Examples for
Discussion}\label{management-decisions-examples-for-discussion}

Drawn from Mid-Atlantic FMPs. Decisions are made through specifications,
frameworks, SSC, amendment and other processes.

\subsection{Definitions:}\label{definitions}

\begin{noteblock}
Routine and Occasional refer to timing of decisions. \textbf{Routine}
decisions are made or reviewed annually or more often across all the
managed stocks, while \textbf{Occasional} decisions are made as needed
to address issues.

\end{noteblock}

\begin{noteblock}
Tactical and Strategic refer to scope of decisions. \textbf{Strategic}
decisions consider long term, high level goals and objectives, while
\textbf{Tactical} decisions implement the strategies in the short term.

\end{noteblock}

\begin{ltgreenbox}

\subsection{Routine/Tactical:
Specifications}\label{routinetactical-specifications}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Catch limits and targets (OFL, ABC, OY, ACL, ACT)\\
\item
  Landings limits (commercial quotas, recreational harvest limits, total
  allowable landings limits, etc.)\\
\item
  Minimum fish sizes (commercial and/or recreational - maximum sizes can
  also be specified for a few species/sectors)\\
\item
  Trip/possession limits (commercial and/or recreational)\\
\item
  Gear restrictions (e.g., commercial minimum mesh sizes, pot/trap
  escape vent size requirements)\\
\item
  Open/closed seasons (recreational; commercial fisheries can close
  in-season if the quota is met before the end of the season)\\
\item
  Reporting requirements specific to each FMP\\
\item
  Commercial bycatch or discard caps (apply to only a subset of MAFMC
  species)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Routine/Tactical: SSC}\label{routinetactical-ssc}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Best scientific information determination (includes scientific working
  group and review panel judgement)
\end{itemize}

\end{ltgreenbox}

\subsection{Occasional, Tactical or Strategic: Framework
Adjustment}\label{occasional-tactical-or-strategic-framework-adjustment}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  ABC control rules\\
\item
  Risk policy\\
\item
  Accountability measures\\
\item
  Permitting restrictions (could require amendment)\\
\item
  Essential Fish Habitat (EFH) and HAPCs\\
\item
  Vessel Monitoring System (VMS) requirements\\
\item
  Spatial management areas (e.g., closed areas, gear restricted areas,
  exemption areas)\\
\item
  Commercial quota allocations\\
\item
  Commercial/recreational allocations (for some FMPs)\\
\item
  Vessel size and horsepower restrictions\\
\item
  Onboard observer requirement (for some FMPs)\\
\item
  Changes to Standardized Bycatch Reporting Method\\
\item
  Deep Sea Coral Protection Zone boundaries and regulations
\end{itemize}

\end{ltbluebox}
