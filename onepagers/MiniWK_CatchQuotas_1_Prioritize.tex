\section{Catch/Quota Setting Step 1: Determine Assessment
Priorities}\label{catchquota-setting-step-1-determine-assessment-priorities}

\begin{noteblock}
Catch and Quota Setting is a multistep process involving decisions by
the Northeast Regional Coordinating Committee (NRCC; including the Mid
Atlantic and New England Councils, Atlantic States Marine Fisheries
Commission, and NOAA's Greater Atlantic Regional Fisheries Office and
Northeast Fisheries Science Center), assessment scientists, assessment
peer reviewers, the Council's Scientific and Statistical Committee
(SSC), and the Council. Information for each stock and fishery is
reviewed and synthesized to determine catch and quota levels that meet
management objectives.

\end{noteblock}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Key question: What additional indicators could inform NRCC
  decisions at the Assessment Prioritization step in the Catch/Quota
  setting process? Could habitat, ecosystem, or socioeconomic indicators
  improve the process?}
\item
  \textbf{Scope: Is a general process for using indicators in the
  Assessment Prioritization step of the Catch/Quota setting process
  preferred?}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What information is used
now?}\label{what-information-is-used-now}

\begin{ltpurplebox}
\textbf{1. Determine Assessment Priorities}

The NRCC prioritizes stocks for assessment based on management needs,
fishery importance, stock status and trend, ecosystem importance,
assessment information, and stock biology. Terms of Reference (ToRs)
ensure the assessment process delivers consistent, management-relevant
outputs.

\end{ltpurplebox}

The NRCC
\href{https://d23h0vhsm26o6d.cloudfront.net/NRCC_Assessment_Process_Version-Jun2025_508.pdf}{prioritization
process} establishes assessment schedules by scoring stocks using
rubrics modified from a
\href{https://www.st.nmfs.noaa.gov/Assets/stock/documents/PrioritizingFishStockAssessments_FinalWeb.pdf}{NMFS
assessment prioritization method}. The NRCC sets, reviews, and adjusts 5
year assessment schedules to align with and address management
priorities throughout the region.

\vspace{2ex}

The NRCC has specified generic Assessment ToRs for
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/s3/2025-07/GenericResearchTrackTermsReference-06252025.pdf}{Research
Track (RT)} and
\href{https://media.fisheries.noaa.gov/2021-01/z.MgtTrackTORs_508_01122021.pdf?null}{Management
Track (MT)} assessments, but these can be amended for specific
processes. The standardized ToRs noted here already permit inclusion of
ecosystem information, implicitly or explicitly:

\vspace{1ex}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  RT Assessment ToR 1 investigates ecosystem and climate influences on
  the stock
\item
  RT ToR 3 or MT ToR 2 presents assessment indices
\item
  RT ToR 5 or MT ToR 4 updates biological references points
\item
  RT ToR 6 or MT ToR 5 develops short term projections
\item
  RT ToR 7 is on research recommendations including how to better
  incorporate ecosystem influences identified under RT ToR 1
\item
  RT ToR 8 develops a backup assessment approach
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2ex}

NRCC guidelines explicitly approve the presentation of contextual
ecosystem information influencing stock dynamics in MT assessments, and
the direct incorporation of ecosystem factors in MT assessment models.

\end{ltbluebox}

\subsection{Information sources for
indicators}\label{information-sources-for-indicators}

Components of the prioritization process use distinct information.

\vspace{1ex}

\href{https://www.mafmc.org/s/2_MAB_RiskAssess_2024.pdf}{Risk
Assessment} indicators by species and sector could inform
\emph{Management needs}

\vspace{1ex}

\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/new-england-mid-atlantic/ecosystems/state-ecosystem-reports-northeast-us-shelf}{State
of the Ecosystem} indicators of performance relative to seafood
production, profits, and recreational opportunities could inform
relative \emph{Fishery importance}. Additional data from the
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/new-england-mid-atlantic/commercial-fishing/quota-monitoring-greater-atlantic-region}{GARFO
Catch and Monitoring System},
\href{https://safis.accsp.org/accsp_prod/f?p=1490:1:6442641492940:::::}{Atlantic
Coastal Cooperative Statistics Programa}, and
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/recreational-fishing-data/introduction-marine-recreational-information-program-data}{Marine
Recreational Information Program} could be included.

\vspace{1ex}

\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/new-england-mid-atlantic/ecosystems/state-ecosystem-reports-northeast-us-shelf}{State
of the Ecosystem}
\href{https://noaa-edab.github.io/catalog/stock_status.html}{stock
status} indicators, individual assessment outputsm and
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/22549}{NEFSC Survey}
based indicators inform \emph{Stock status and trend}

\vspace{1ex}

\href{https://fwdp.shinyapps.io/tm2020/}{NEFSC Food Habits data},
\href{https://www.vims.edu/research/units/programs/multispecies_fisheries_research/neamap/}{NEAMAP
Survey and Food Habits data},
\href{https://nrha.shinyapps.io/dataexplorer/}{NRHA Data Explorer}
predator prey information, and
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/new-england-mid-atlantic/ecosystems/state-ecosystem-reports-northeast-us-shelf}{State
of the Ecosystem} indicators could inform \emph{Ecosystem importance}

\vspace{1ex}

Current assessments,
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/new-england-mid-atlantic/ecosystems/ecosystem-and-socioeconomic-profile-development-and-reports}{Ecosystem
and Socioeconomic Profiles}, catch and survey information sources listed
above could inform \emph{Assessment information}

\vspace{1ex}

\href{https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0146756}{Climate
Vulnerability Analysis},
\href{https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/new-england-mid-atlantic/ecosystems/ecosystem-and-socioeconomic-profile-development-and-reports}{Ecosystem
and Socioeconomic Profiles}, and biological data included in assessments
could inform \emph{Stock biology}

\subsubsection{Potential Pathways}\label{potential-pathways}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  NRCC prioritization could apply standard indicators at either the
  stock or full ecosystem level
\item
  Specific RT or MT ToRs could be developed addressing ecosystem change
\end{itemize}

\begin{ltpurplebox}

\subsection{Information gaps}\label{information-gaps}

The specific assessment prioritization scoring rubrics applied by the
NRCC would need to be provided in order to evaluate the potential for
improved indicator use in this process.

\end{ltpurplebox}
