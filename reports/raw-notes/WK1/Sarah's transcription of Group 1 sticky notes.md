**Discussion 2 Decision Priorities:** 

Quotas→amount of allowable catch and Measures→ how to catch are fundamental to the process. Highest priorities, no fishery without these

Quotas

* Council decisions here constrained by MSA and decisions earlier in the process, only discard mortality is debatable  
* SSC ABC decision sets cap, look to that process  
* SSC could list specific indicators used  
* SSC highlight key uncertainties in ABC (does but not always prioritized)   
* Find indicators to address the key uncertainties   
* Could SSC use habitat forecasts? ToR for SSC?

Measures (season open close, bag limit, size limit, etc: rec but can generalize)

* Objective: maximize fishing opportunities across species and fleets  
* Need information on what else is open when and where if closing something important  
* Ideally overlap in available opportunities so pressure is not all on one  
* Indicator of opportunity, may accept higher risk if making more opportunities  
* Number and diversity of opportunities available to a fleet sector  
* Opportunity tradeoffs important to understand  
* Need info from MAFMC managed species measures as well as NEFMC, ASMFC, HMS

Spatial Measures

* Evaluate effectiveness of current spatial measures  
* Indicator of overlap: fisheries and species distributions  
  * Assess fishery and other ocean user conflicts  
  * Evaluate societal tradeoffs  
    

Planning for the future

* Implementation plan and annual priorities could benefit from some indicators to ensure up and coming issues are addressed  
* Hard to change the plan once in place

Timing of information to Council

* Include indicators in decision memo or earlier in the process  
* Accelerate the science process for indicators  
* Indicators could be integrated in fishery performance reports or other reports where they address a particular topic or where FPR info explains trends in indicators  
* Metric of overall rec effort can help prioritize bycatch discussion
