2. What is an [indicator](https://sgaichas.github.io/HSpresentations/20250929_Definitions_Gaichas.html#2)? (4 slides, including SMART concept, 5 minutes with questions)  
*  Dr. Gaichas  
  * What is an indicator:  
    * Tells us something about where we are relative to our goals or limits, or tells us about context we are working within  
  * Indicators and thresholds: stock status, fishing mortality, revenue, effort  
  * Risks to meeting objectives  
    * Temperature sensitivity, offshore wind footprint  
  *  SMART indicators: Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound  
    * Specific: clearly defines  
    * Measurable: quantitative or qualitative data  
    * Achievable: detect changes from management; can we achieve objectives  
    * Relevant: Accurately reflect changes in the underlying process. Can be directly linked to management process objective  
    * Time-bound: How often is it updated?
